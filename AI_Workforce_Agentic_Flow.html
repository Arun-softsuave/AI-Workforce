It remembers the conversation: "1" or "yes" goes to what it asked last. It never guesses otherwise; if it can't tell, it asks one short question.

Always on

  • Teams only: no desktop copies or fallback; failed messages are retried on Teams
  • 24 hours: no quiet hours, while the computer is on
  • 10:00 check-up: what's waiting for you, running, done, failed and new, plus connection health. If the computer was off, it's sent when it's back on.

Claude Code

  • Workforce projects: tickets and work requests are handed over, never coded in your session
  • Status: "status of KAN-42?" gets the same answer as in Teams, e.g. "implementing and testing, attempt 2 of 3"
  • Approvals: only in Teams; Claude Code shows status
  • Other projects: work normally

Guards

  • Models: small for triage, medium for chat replies, building and reviews, large to plan and to lead work across repositories
  • Budget: daily token limit pauses work
  • Secrets: scanned before push, kept in the OS vault
  • Attribution: your git identity, no AI mentions
  • Crash-safe: every step is saved; it resumes and publishes exactly once
Everything runs on your computer. Work happens only in separate git worktrees. Draft PRs only; nothing is ever merged or deployed.
